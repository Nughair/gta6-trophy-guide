    Roadmap to the platinum

    Play the story first and enjoy it — most of Leonida opens up as you go. The seven flagged missables are pinned at the top of this roadmap with warning styling so you meet them before their moments pass; everything else can be mopped up in free roam afterwards.

    Missables — read before you play

    These seven trophies are flagged because their official descriptions tie the chance to a particular story moment or partnership beat. Treat each one as a risk read from the description, not a confirmed hard lock: if the moment passes, mission replay is the recovery we recommend. Windows below are our reading of the descriptions — see each guide for the reasoning and its sources.

    Save & sources

    Your progress lives only in this browser (localStorage): the earned set, every counter and the spoiler setting. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — the app makes no network requests at all.

    Where the data comes from

    Trophy names, official descriptions and grades are the published PS5 list, cited per trophy in each guide’s Sources list (Exophase’s Grand Theft Auto VI (PS5) trophy list). Counted targets are cross-checked against PSNProfiles’ Grand Theft Auto VI trophy list. Every strategy note is our own advice, worked out from the official description — the game is not out yet, so none of it has been checked in play, and we say so on every trophy rather than dressing guesses up as facts.

    Spoiler shield

    With the shield on, story trophies show masked names and descriptions until you earn them, so the checklist itself cannot spoil the plot. Guides stay readable — open one only when you want it.
